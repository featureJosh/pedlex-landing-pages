# User agent review: Racking FR, round 2 (2026-10-30)

**Role:** User agent, the impatient professional (CLAUDE.md §4). I'm an operations manager at a mid-size manufacturer in Beauce. Between two meetings I tapped a Facebook video ad: « Votre entrepôt est plein… ou simplement mal optimisé? »
**Build tested:** the existing `_site/` on `main` (not rebuilt), served on :8096. I tested `/racking/`, `/racking/video/` (hero B), `/racking/merci/` and glanced at `/racking/en/`.
**Runs:**
1. **Mobile:** 390×844, DSF 2, touch, Android UA, fr-CA. This was a real first visit with no consent set and UTMs in the URL. I screenshotted the first screen at 1 s and again at 5 s, tapped « Refuser », then skimmed in flicks of 560–740px with a 0.9 s pause, taking a viewport screenshot at each stop. Then I went back to the top, tapped the hero CTA and filled the form with a bad email (« m.gagnon@usinagebeauce »). I submitted anyway, fixed the email, submitted again and landed on the thank-you page. After that I looked at hero B and the EN page. Screenshots `m01`–`m27`, log `qa/shots/user-r2/run-mobile.log`.
2. **Desktop:** 1440×900, mouse wheel, same skim. I clicked the « Pourquoi Pedlex » play button, opened the FAQ answer I care about and took the header CTA. In the form I typed a 9-digit phone, submitted anyway, tried a phone with « poste 22 », fixed it and submitted. Then I followed « Retour à la page Palettiers » and looked at hero B and the EN page. Screenshots `d01`–`d23`, log `run-desktop.log`.
3. **Stats check** at 1440×900, 1366×768 and 1920×1080 after declining cookies, without scrolling (`d24`–`d26`).

All screenshots are in `qa/shots/user-r2/`. The scripts are outside the repo (session scratchpad). There were **no console errors and no horizontal overflow** on either run.

---

## Verdict in one paragraph

This is better than round 1, and on mobile it's now clearly better. The first phone screen finally shows racks, the page is about 1,600px shorter, and the form is still the best part of the page. I'd still convert, because the CTA follows me everywhere and the form is short and forgiving. Two things knock it down a notch for a sceptic like me. First, on my laptop the hero's proof row reads **« 0 à 0 % · 0 ans · 0+ »** until I scroll. Zero years of expertise is the first number I see, and it's comical. Second, the page has a section titled « Ce que nos clients disent de Pedlex » that contains **nothing**, plus a visible internal note saying the testimonials are still to come. The design says « serious modern company » and those two spots say « unfinished ». Fix the counter now. Don't launch with the empty testimonials.

## 1. The 5-second impression

**Mobile** (`m01` at 1 s, `m02` at 5 s, `m03` after « Refuser »):
- **Understood immediately:** racking, a full or badly organised warehouse, a free consultation. The H1 is the ad's line word for word, over a photo of real blue and orange racking. That's the fix I asked for in round 1, and it works. I knew in 2 seconds I was in the right place.
- **Still in the way:** the « Votre vie privée » sheet is **250px tall** (y = 578 → 828) and sits over the hero CTA (603 → 669) and the whole proof row. I tapped « Refuser » without reading, like everyone does.
- **After the banner:** photo, H1, one line of lead, CTA + phone button, then « 30 à 50 % · 50 ans · 10 000+ ». That's a good first screen. « 10 000+ entreprises servies » is the number that tells me these guys aren't a garage operation.
- **Odd:** I count **three « Consultation gratuite »** in one screen: the top bar link, the header button and the hero button (`m03`). That's one too many. It reads a bit pushy, and the top bar on mobile has lost its useful line (« Entreprise familiale… depuis 1976 · Réponse… 1 jour ouvrable ») and kept only the duplicate CTA.
- The hero photo is soft at phone resolution, like smeared JPEG (known, CLIENT-19). On a phone I barely notice it.

**Desktop** (`d01`, `d02`, `d24`):
- A strong screen: big headline left, a wall of real racking right, the CTA and the phone number side by side, and « Entreprise familiale québécoise depuis 1976 · Réponse d'un expert en 1 jour ouvrable » in the top bar. In 5 seconds I knew who they are, what they do and how fast they answer.
- **Then I read the bottom row: « 0 à 0 % d'espace récupéré », « 0 ans d'expertise », « 0+ entreprises servies »** (`d24`). It sits there for as long as I don't scroll. Same at 1366×768 (`d25`). Only at 1920×1080 (`d26`) did it count up. On the most common laptop screens, the first proof I get says zero.

## 2. Where I hesitated, got bored or confused

1. **The zeros on desktop** (above). I literally did a double take. If I were less patient I'd think the page was broken.
2. **« Ce que nos clients disent de Pedlex. »** (`m11`, `d14`): three grey cards with « Témoignage client à venir. », « Nom du client », « Poste, entreprise », a « PLACEHOLDER » tag, and under them « Placeholder : témoignages réels à fournir par Pedlex… ». 4. **The problem section on mobile** (`m04`–`m06`): four cards about capacity, access, flow and odd-sized products. They're tighter than round 1 (the section went from 1,524 to 1,176px, with icons now inline). I still skimmed past all four, because I know what racking does. It's acceptable now, not a bounce risk.
5. **FAQ: « Est-ce que Pedlex peut optimiser un système de palettiers existant? »** (`d15`) still only says « Oui. Pedlex peut analyser votre configuration actuelle… ». That's the question my boss would ask (we already have racks). Can you reuse my uprights? Do you re-certify damaged frames? The answer doesn't say.

What pulled me through: the gallery photos (`m06`, `m07`, `d05`; the big selective-rack aisle shot is excellent), the process section with the installer on the lift behind it (`m08`), and the « Sécurité et conformité » card that finally says something concrete: certified installers, engineer inspection, **CSA A344**. ## 5. Form friction

Still very good. The essentials:
- **Length:** 5 required fields + 1 optional box. A one-screen 2-column grid on desktop, about 1.5 screens on mobile. It feels short.
- **Keyboards and autofill:** `type="email"` with `autocapitalize="off"` and `spellcheck="false"`; `type="tel"` + `inputmode="tel"`; `autocomplete` = name / organization / email / tel / address-level2. My phone would fill four of the five fields.
- **Mistake 1, mobile, bad email** (`m18`–`m21`): the error appeared when I left the field, in red with an icon, right under the field: « Entrez une adresse courriel valide (ex. : nom@entreprise.com). » Submitting anyway showed a summary box (« Veuillez corriger les champs indiqués. ») and put focus on the email field. Adding « .ca » cleared the error as I typed. One nit: the message breaks as « (ex. » / « : nom@entreprise.com) », with the colon starting a line (`m18`).
- **Mistake 2, desktop, 9-digit phone** (`d17`, `d18`): « Entrez un numéro de téléphone valide (10 chiffres). » On submit, focus went to the phone field. Clear and fair.
- **My office line with an extension, « (418) 555-1234 poste 22 », is rejected** as invalid. That's how I give my number to every supplier. I'd delete the extension and grumble.
- **Submit:** `dataLayer` got `generate_lead`, then the browser redirected to `/racking/merci/`. The consent line under the button is short and plain.
- The submit label still wraps to two lines on mobile (« Demander ma / consultation gratuite », `m22`). It doesn't bother me much, but the problem-section CTA does the same (`m04`), while the hero uses a short one-line label.

## Findings

### USER-1 · blocker · hero
**What:** The proof row counts up only when its top is above 85% of the viewport (`rootMargin: "0px 0px -15% 0px"` in `stats-counter.js`), and the script first sets every number to « 0 ». At 1440×900 the row's top is at y = 797 (the trigger line is 765). At 1366×768 it's at 693 (trigger 653). So after the cookie choice, the first screen reads **« 0 à 0 % d'espace récupéré · 0 ans d'expertise · 0+ entreprises servies »** and stays that way until the visitor scrolls. It counts correctly at 1920×1080 and on mobile 390×844. Hero B (`/racking/video/`) and EN show the same thing.
**Where:** 1440×900 `qa/shots/user-r2/d24-stats-after-decline-1440x900.png`, 1366×768 `d25-stats-after-decline-1366x768.png`, hero B `d21-video-hero-first-screen.png`; correct at 1920 `d26-…1920x1080.png`.
**Why it matters:** The first proof a laptop visitor sees says « zero years, zero clients ». That's the opposite of the message, at the 5-second moment.
**Suggested fix:** In `src/scripts/sections/stats-counter.js`, don't zero the numbers until the row actually starts animating (set « 0 » inside `run()`, not at ready). Also, if the group is already within the viewport at load (`getBoundingClientRect().top < innerHeight`), run immediately instead of waiting for the -15% margin. A margin of `0px` with `threshold: 0` would also do. The HTML already holds the final values, so the worst case should be « no animation », never « 0 ».

### USER-2 · major · reviews
**What:** The « 05 · Témoignages / Ce que nos clients disent de Pedlex. » section shows three cards reading « Témoignage client à venir. », « Nom du client », « Poste, entreprise », each with a « PLACEHOLDER » tag, plus the visible note « Placeholder : témoignages réels à fournir par Pedlex (nom, poste, entreprise, citation approuvée). » It's 589px tall on mobile and 694px on desktop. EN is the same (« Client testimonial to come. »).
**Where:** 390 `qa/shots/user-r2/m11-skim-why+reviews+faq.png`; 1440 `d14-reviews.png`; EN `d23-en-reviews.png`.
**Why it matters:** An empty testimonials block reads as « nobody vouches for them ». It removes trust rather than adding none, and it must not reach a paid-traffic launch.
**Suggested fix:** Known placeholder, so this is a go-live gate. Add a content flag (e.g. `c.reviews.hidden` or « render only if no item has `placeholder: true` ») so `sections/reviews.njk` drops out of the page when the content isn't real. Separately, `claude/context.md` lists two catalogue quotes Pedlex already published (Benoît Boulerice, Groupe Ricochet; André Lapointe, Globocam). Ask Pedlex to approve reusing them (PROJECT.md §9) rather than waiting for new ones.

### USER-3 · major · hero (cookie banner, <48rem)
**What:** On a first mobile visit the « Votre vie privée » sheet is 250px tall (y = 578 → 828 at 390×844). It covers the hero CTA (603 → 669), the phone button and the whole proof row. This is unchanged from round 1 (USER-5 / QA-2, still open in §8).
**Where:** 390 `qa/shots/user-r2/m01-first-screen-1s.png`, `m02-first-screen-5s.png`.
**Why it matters:** The first-screen CTA and the three proof numbers are hidden at the moment the visitor decides whether to stay.
**Suggested fix:** As approved in §8 (USER-5 / QA-2): a compact bar of 150px or less below 30rem, with a visually hidden title, one-line text plus « En savoir plus », and « Refuser » / « Accepter » side by side with equal weight, in `components/cookie-banner` CSS.

### USER-4 · major · gallery / proof
**What:** The page still has no named project and no result. The only figure is « 30 à 50 % … dans certains projets ». Gallery captions are product types (« Palettiers sélectifs », « Cantilever pour produits longs »…). Pedlex's own 2026 catalogue already publishes named results: STM (mezzanine, storage ×3), Groupe Ricochet (4,000+ pallet positions, 32 ft racking in 3 weeks), Airbus (racking + cantilever, 26 ft), Traction Joliette (storage ×2), per `claude/context.md`.
**Where:** 390 `m06`, `m07`; 1440 `qa/shots/user-r2/d05-skim-gallery.png`.
**Why it matters:** A sceptical buyer needs one « client X got Y » to move from « credible » to « call today ». Logos alone don't say what changed.
**Suggested fix:** CLIENT-5/USER-3 was declined in round 1 to avoid inventing captions. These figures aren't invented: they're Pedlex's published catalogue facts. Ask Pedlex (§9) to OK one or two of them and the matching photos, then use the gallery `detail` support (client · city · result) on 1–2 slides, or add a single result line under the gallery head in `src/content/racking/fr.json`.

### USER-5 · minor · why
**What:** The HQ/fleet photo has a large round play button in the centre. Clicking or tapping it does nothing: the URL is unchanged, there's no message, and `.why__play` has `cursor: auto`. The only hint is the small dashed tag « Vidéo Pedlex à venir ».
**Where:** 1440 `qa/shots/user-r2/d12-why-video-frame.png`, `d13-why-video-after-click.png`; 390 `m09-skim-process+why.png`.
**Why it matters:** A play button that doesn't play feels broken, and it sits in the section meant to build trust.
**Suggested fix:** Until the video exists, render the frame without the `.why__play` glyph (in `sections/why.njk`, make the play span conditional on a real `c.why.video.src`, not the placeholder). Keep the photo + caption, which work fine on their own.

### USER-6 · minor · header (global)
**What:** The phone number no longer stays on screen while scrolling. It now lives only in `.topbar`, which scrolls away. The sticky `site-header` holds just the logo + CTA. On desktop no `tel:` link is visible from y ≈ 700 to the FAQ/form (about 5,000px). On mobile none is visible from the hero to the consultation section. Round 1 had a phone icon or number in the sticky header.
**Where:** 1440 `d03`–`d09` (`telVisible: 0` in `run-desktop.log`); 390 `m04`–`m12`.
**Why it matters:** B2B buyers who prefer to call lose the one-tap option for most of the page.
**Suggested fix:** Add the existing phone link (QA-12 `phoneLink` macro) to `site-header__inner`: a 44×44 icon below 48rem and the number at wider sizes, next to the CTA.

### USER-7 · minor · hero / topbar (<48rem)
**What:** The first mobile screen has three identical CTAs within about 650px: the top-bar link « Consultation gratuite → » (y 0–44), the header button « Consultation gratuite » (56–96) and the hero button « Consultation gratuite » (603–669). On mobile the top bar hides its message (« Entreprise familiale québécoise depuis 1976 · Réponse d'un expert en 1 jour ouvrable ») and keeps only the duplicate CTA, the phone and EN.
**Where:** 390 `qa/shots/user-r2/m03-after-decline.png`; EN `m27-en-first-screen.png`.
**Why it matters:** Repeating the same button three times reads as pushy. Meanwhile the strongest trust line (family company since 1976, reply in 1 day) is the thing that got dropped.
**Suggested fix:** Below 48rem, hide `.topbar__cta` (the header button already does that job) and show a short top-bar message instead, e.g. the bold « Entreprise familiale québécoise depuis 1976 » part of `.topbar__text`, next to the phone and EN.

### USER-8 · minor · problem, consultation (<48rem)
**What:** In-page CTAs wrap to two lines on mobile: « Demandez votre consultation / gratuite » in the problem section (`m04`) and the submit button « Demander ma / consultation gratuite » with the arrow badge to the right (`m14`, `m22`). The hero and header use the one-line short label « Consultation gratuite ». This carries over USER-10 / CLIENT-14.
**Where:** 390 `qa/shots/user-r2/m04-skim-hero+trust+problem.png`, `m14-skim-consultation.png`, `m22-before-submit.png`.
**Why it matters:** The buttons look inconsistent from one section to the next, and the two-line ones read heavier.
**Suggested fix:** Per §8 USER-10: tighter `.btn--lg` mobile padding in `button.css`. Alternatively, use the macro's existing long/short label pair (`btn__label--long` / `--short`) for the problem CTA and the submit button below 30rem.

### USER-9 · minor · consultation (<48rem)
**What:** Tapping the hero CTA scrolls to the top of `#consultation`. The visitor sees the H2, the pitch paragraph and the three ticks again, and the focused « Prénom et nom » field sits at 688–736px in an 844px viewport. That's better than round 1 (788–836), but once the keyboard opens the field would be covered or shoved. This carries over USER-6 / QA-1.
**Where:** 390 `qa/shots/user-r2/m17-after-hero-cta-tap.png` (log: `active: lead-name, top: 688`).
**Why it matters:** The visitor came to fill the form, not to re-read the pitch, and that's where the keyboard friction starts.
**Suggested fix:** Per §8 QA-1/USER-6: below 64rem, `scroll-to.js` targets the form card (`[data-lead-form]` or the `.consult` form wrapper) with the header offset instead of the section top.

### USER-10 · minor · consultation (form)
**What:** « (418) 555-1234 poste 22 » is rejected as invalid (`aria-invalid="true"`). This carries over QA-10.
**Where:** 1440 (`run-desktop.log`, line « extension true »).
**Why it matters:** Many plant and office managers give their number with an extension. Being told it's invalid is friction right at the conversion.
**Suggested fix:** Per §8 QA-10: in `lead-form.js`, strip a trailing `poste|ext\.?|x` + digits before counting the 10 digits, and keep the full string in the submitted value.

### USER-11 · minor · faq
**What:** « Est-ce que Pedlex peut optimiser un système de palettiers existant? » is answered with « Oui. Pedlex peut analyser votre configuration actuelle afin d'identifier des moyens de mieux exploiter votre espace et de l'adapter à vos opérations. » That's a yes without substance (reuse of components? inspection / re-certification of damaged frames?). This carries over from round 1.
**Where:** 1440 `qa/shots/user-r2/d15-faq-existing-open.png`.
**Why it matters:** Most buyers already have racks. This is the question that decides whether they call at all.
**Suggested fix:** Copy, not build: log it as a client question in PROJECT.md §9. Pedlex's services already include inspection and CSA A344 certification (see the « Sécurité et conformité » card), so one more concrete sentence is likely approvable.

### USER-12 · minor · hero (variant B, `/racking/video/`)
**What:** The placeholder clip has centred text baked in (« PLACEHOLDER / Vidéo d'arrière-plan à venir / Installateur Pedlex · 2–3 plans de 2–3 s »). At 390px, `object-fit: cover` crops it, and the visible part (« PLAC… / Vidéo d'arr… / …stallateur Pedle… ») sits directly behind « mal optimisé? ». On desktop it sits mid-right, competing with the H1. As a first screen, B reads as « under construction » next to A.
**Where:** 390 `qa/shots/user-r2/m25-video-hero-first-screen.png`; 1440 `d21-video-hero-first-screen.png`.
**Why it matters:** Anyone reviewing A vs B on a phone will judge B by this crop. The real clip will hit the same problem if it has a centred subject or text.
**Suggested fix:** Known placeholder: keep A as the default. Make the placeholder clip text-free (the chip already labels it). For the real clip, brief a mobile-safe framing (subject in the upper third, no burned-in text) and check it at 390px before testing B.

### USER-13 · nit · consultation (form)
**What:** The email error breaks as « Entrez une adresse courriel valide (ex. » / « : nom@entreprise.com). », with the colon starting the second line. The string in `data-error-email` has a plain space before « : ». FR typography isn't applied to `_shared` error strings rendered into attributes.
**Where:** 390 `qa/shots/user-r2/m18-bad-email-blur.png`.
**Why it matters:** It's a small FR typography slip right where the user is paying attention.
**Suggested fix:** Run the `_shared/fr.json` error strings through the same FR typography pass as page copy in `src/_data/landings.js`, so they get a narrow no-break space before « : ». Alternatively, rephrase as « (p. ex. nom@entreprise.com) », which is also the more standard Québec form (lead copy change, log in §9).

### USER-15 · nit · footer
**What:** The footer has « Découvrir pedlex.com ↗ », which sends paid traffic to the old site, the one this page is meant to move away from.
**Where:** 390 `qa/shots/user-r2/m16-skim-consultation.png`.

**Suggested fix:** The lead's call: keep it if Pedlex wants it, but consider removing it from the campaign footer (or at least not giving it the bold link styling) in the footer partial.
