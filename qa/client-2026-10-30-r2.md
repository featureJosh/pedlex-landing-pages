## Verdict: **approve with changes**

This is the round where it starts to look like *our next website*, not a tidier pedlex.com. The dark photo hero with the orange-and-blue racking is the strongest first screen Pedlex has ever had online. The square shapes feel like us, the sticky header is calm and the gallery finally lets the installations speak. We would show this internally today, placeholders included, as long as Luxy fixes CLIENT-20 first.

We would **not** launch it yet. One thing is broken on screen and four things are missing or need our confirmation:
1. **On a normal laptop, the hero says « 0 ans d'expertise » and « 0+ entreprises »** until the visitor scrolls (CLIENT-20). That's the first thing a plant manager sees from our paid ad. Fix it before anyone else sees this.
2. **The 50th anniversary disappeared** from the page in this round (CLIENT-21). 2026 is our 50th year; the catalogue is built around it.
3. **Testimonials are placeholders** (CLIENT-22). That's fine for an internal mockup, but the section cannot go live empty. We owe Luxy the quotes.
4. **« 30 à 50 % »** is a bigger claim than Maïeutyk's original « jusqu'à 40 % » and still has no project behind it (CLIENT-23).
5. **The logo row still undersells us** (CLIENT-24): six mid-size names, while we're cleared to show Bombardier, Air Canada, Hydro-Québec and CAE.

## Does it feel like Pedlex?
- **Industrial, confident, precise: yes.** Real steel, real warehouses, navy and white, square corners, numbered sections, one action. Nothing playful, nothing "startup". The installer-on-the-lift band behind the 1-2-3 process is exactly the image of Pedlex we want: people, safety, precision (`scroll-1440-04.png`).
- **A 50-year family company: only partly.** « Entreprise familiale québécoise depuis 1976 » is in the top bar on desktop and under the HQ/fleet photo in « Pourquoi Pedlex ». That HQ photo with our trucks is a great choice. But on phones the top-bar message is hidden, and the « 50 ans · 1976–2026 » chip from round 1 is gone (CLIENT-21).
- **« Défie l'espace »: not yet.** Our slogan appears nowhere. It waits for the vector logo (CLIENT-31).
- **Our next look, not the old site: yes.** Next to pedlex.com (`ref-pedlex-com-1440-top.png`: webinar bar, full menu, shop button, heavy wide caps, chat bubble), this reads as a new brand expression. The H1 in Scania Sans regular, mixed case, is lighter than our old caps headlines. It feels modern, and the caps Headline cut on the small labels keeps the family link. We accept it. It's also clearly not Federal Steel (dark/amber, collage, two CTAs).
- **One section still feels like the old brochure:** « 01 Optimisez votre entreposage » with four white icon cards (`scroll-1440-01.png`, `scroll-390-02.png`). It's fine, but it's the least "new" block and the longest on phones. When the new photos arrive, this is where we'd want one.

## Québec French and tone
- **Natural Québec French throughout:** « courriel », « témoins », « Sans frais », « palettiers », « échéancier », « Licence RBQ », « boul. du Golf ». FR typography is right: narrow spaces before « ? » and « % », curly apostrophes, « 1 888 733-5399 ».
- **Tone:** professional, reassuring and expert. The new microcopy reads naturally: « Ce que nos clients disent de Pedlex. », « Vos questions, nos réponses. », « Vous préférez nous parler directement ? »
- **Anglicism:** « Placeholder » appears in the testimonial tags, the testimonial note and the variant-B hero. For an internal showing at Pedlex, « À venir » reads better (CLIENT-22, CLIENT-28).
- **Repetition:** « consultation gratuite » appears 11 times in the page text, three of them in the first phone screen (CLIENT-32). That's the campaign, so we only flag the first screen.

## Change requests

### CLIENT-20 · blocker · hero
**What:** On page load, the proof strip at the bottom of the hero reads « 0 à 0 % », « 0 ans d'expertise en entreposage », « 0+ entreprises servies au Québec et ailleurs ». It stays at zero until the visitor scrolls. Measured after 5 s with no scroll: **1366×768 → 0/0/0/0** (strip top at 693px), **1440×900 → 0/0/0/0** (strip top at 797px). At 1920×1080 and 390×844 the numbers do appear, but 390 only barely (strip top 717px vs. the 717px trigger line).
**Where:** 1366 `qa/shots/client-r2/stats-zero-on-load-1366.png`; 1440 `stats-zero-on-load-1440.png`, crop `zoom-hero-stats-1440.png`
**Why it matters:** The most common office laptop sizes greet a paid-ad visitor with « 0 ans d'expertise ». A visitor who doesn't scroll leaves thinking that's our number.
**Suggested fix:** In `src/scripts/sections/stats-counter.js`, the IntersectionObserver uses `rootMargin: "0px 0px -15% 0px"`, so a strip sitting in the bottom 15% of the first screen never fires. Run the counter immediately if the group is already in the viewport at `ready` (any intersection), or drop the bottom margin for the hero strip. Re-check at 1280×720, 1366×768, 1440×900 and 390×844.

### CLIENT-21 · major · hero / topbar (50th anniversary, family story)
**What:** The « 50 ans · 1976–2026 » chip approved in round 1 (CLIENT-9, PROJECT.md §8) is no longer rendered anywhere. `ui.since` still exists in `src/content/_shared/fr.json` but no template uses it. On phones, the top bar shows only « Consultation gratuite → », the phone and EN; the message « Entreprise familiale québécoise depuis 1976 » is hidden. The first mention of the family company on a phone comes at about 5 400px, under the video in « Pourquoi Pedlex ».
**Where:** 1440 `first-screen-a-1440.png`; 390 `first-screen-a-390.png`, `scroll-390-06.png`
**Why it matters:** 2026 is our 50th year and our whole catalogue is built around it. « 50 ans » in the proof strip reads as a statistic, not as an anniversary or a family story. This is a regression of an approved note.
**Suggested fix:** Bring `c.ui.since` back as a quiet anniversary mark in the hero (e.g. a second chip beside « Maximisez votre capacité d'entreposage », or a small line above the proof strip). On phones (<30rem), show a short top-bar message (« Entreprise familiale depuis 1976 ») instead of the « Consultation gratuite » link, which duplicates the header button (see CLIENT-32). Log it in TIMELINE.
