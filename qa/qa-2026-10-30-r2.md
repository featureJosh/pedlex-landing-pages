## Findings

### QA-1 · blocker · hero
**What:** On most desktop, laptop and tablet first screens the proof strip reads **« 0 à 0 % · 0 ans · 0+ »** and stays that way until the visitor scrolls. Measured after 2.5 s idle: 1440×900, 1366×768, 1280×720, 1024×768, 768×1024 and 390×664 all show zeros. Only 1920×1080 and 390×844 count up. `stats-counter.js` sets every figure to `0` at ready, but the IntersectionObserver uses `rootMargin: "0px 0px -15% 0px"`, and the strip sits in the bottom 15% of the hero (for example 1440×900: strip at y 797–852, trigger line 765).
**Where:** 1366×768 `qa/shots/qa-r2/ix-counter-zero-1366x768.png`; 1440×900 `qa/shots/qa-r2/ix-firstscreen-1440.png`
**Why it matters:** The company's three key proof points read as « 0 ans d'expertise » on the first screen of a typical laptop. It looks broken and false in the client demo.
**Suggested fix:** In `src/scripts/sections/stats-counter.js`, don't zero figures at ready. Set them to 0 inside `run()` when counting starts. Also use `rootMargin: "0px"` (or `threshold: 0.5`), or start right away when the group is already inside the viewport on load.

### QA-2 · major · page (mobile length)
**What:** At 390 the page is **8 874px** (budget ≤ 7 500, +18%) and the first form field is at **7 084px** (budget ≤ 5 000, +42%). At 360 the numbers are 9 213 / 7 423. The biggest costs at 390: consultation 1 682 (≈ 600px of intro, lead and reassurance before the card), why 1 218 (head → video → 4 reasons → certs; the head-to-list distance alone is 351px), problem 1 176 (4 stacked cards), gallery 1 059, process 804, hero 800, **footer 708** (single column).
**Where:** 390 `qa/shots/qa-r2/full-390.png`; 360 `qa/shots/qa-r2/full-360.png`
**Why it matters:** Raffi's top priority is page length and mobile. The form, which is the one action, sits about 8.4 screens down.
**Suggested fix:** (a) Footer below 48rem: 2 columns for address and contact (`components/footer.css`), saving about 200px. (b) Why on phones: drop the video caption, or put the reasons in a 2-column grid of titles only (the text is already in the FAQ and process). (c) Problem on phones: titles only, or a 2×2 compact grid. (d) Consultation on phones: hide the intro lead below 40rem (the reassurance list already says it). (e) Trim the 4–5-line H2s (see QA-19). The lead decides which ones.

### QA-3 · major · consultation
**What:** On a short phone viewport, every CTA lands on the section intro and the focused field is off-screen. At 390×664 (an iPhone with browser chrome) `#lead-name` ends up at y 701–749 with a viewport height of 664, for all 5 CTAs. At 390×844 it sits at y 688–736, at the very bottom, under a screen of intro copy. This carries over round-1 QA-1 / USER-6.
**Where:** 390×664 `qa/shots/qa-r2/ix-cta-landing-390x664.png`
**Why it matters:** The keyboard opens on a field the visitor can't see, which is the worst moment to add friction.
**Suggested fix:** Below 64rem, scroll to `.consult__card` instead of the section. In `scroll-to.js`, prefer `target.querySelector("[data-scroll-anchor]")` and put `data-scroll-anchor` on the card. Alternatively, focus without `preventScroll` once the smooth scroll ends.

### QA-4 · major · global (top bar, transparent header)
**What:** The focus ring is close to invisible on the navy top bar and on the transparent header over the hero photo. `:focus-visible` uses `--color-focus` (#2F5091) on `--navy-950` (#0A2A45), which is **1.9:1**. The white override only applies inside `.section--dark` and `.site-footer`, and the top bar and header are neither. This affects the « Consultation gratuite » link, the phone link, « EN » and the header logo.
**Where:** 390 `qa/shots/qa-r2/crop-focus-topbar-lang-390.png`, `qa/shots/qa-r2/ix-focus-390-2.png`, `qa/shots/qa-r2/crop-focus-logo-390.png`; 1440 `qa/shots/qa-r2/ix-focus-1440-4.png`
**Why it matters:** These are the first four tab stops on the page. A keyboard user can't see where they are (WCAG 2.4.7, 1.4.11).
**Suggested fix:** In `02-base.css`, add `.topbar :focus-visible` and `.site-header:not([data-scrolled]):not(.site-header--solid) :focus-visible` to the white-outline rule.

### QA-5 · major · trust
**What:** The logo marquee (40s loop, infinite) can't be paused by touch or keyboard. It has 0 focusable elements, so `:focus-within` never fires, and there's no `[data-marquee-toggle]`. Only mouse hover pauses it. This carries over round-1 QA-4.
**Where:** 390 / 1440 `qa/shots/qa-r2/section-trust-390.png`
**Why it matters:** WCAG 2.2.2: moving content longer than 5s needs a pause mechanism. PROJECT §5 asks for « a pausable logo marquee ».
**Suggested fix:** Add a small pause/play button (`data-marquee-toggle`, `aria-pressed`, ≥ 44px, square) after the rail in `trust.njk`, wired in `trust-marquee.js` with `animation-play-state`.

### QA-6 · major · cookie banner

### QA-7 · major · global (touch targets)
**What:** Controls below the 44px rule (CLAUDE.md §2):

### QA-8 · major · global (type scale: h3)
**What:** The h3 level doesn't follow the documented scale. PROJECT §5 says « h3 18–22 » (`--fs-h3`), but **every h3** (problem cards, process steps, why reasons) uses `--fs-md`: **16.14px at 390** (the same as the 16px body and the FAQ question) and 18px at 1440. `--fs-h3` is only used by the trust **h2** and the merci page. There are also two h3 treatments: `.card__title` is w600 with letter-spacing 0, while `.process__title` is w700 with -0.02em (from the base h3). The trust h2 is the only h2 off `--fs-h2` (18–22px, w600).
**Where:** 390 `qa/shots/qa-r2/section-problem-390.png`, `qa/shots/qa-r2/section-process-390.png`; 1440 `qa/shots/qa-r2/section-why-1440.png`
**Why it matters:** Raffi's top priority is type consistency. On phones the card and step titles have no size step above the body text.
**Suggested fix:** In `02-base.css`, set `h3 { font-size: var(--fs-h3) }`. Remove the `font-size: var(--fs-md)` and `letter-spacing: 0` overrides from `card.css` and `process.css`, and use one weight (`--fw-bold`). Either update the spec to « h3 16–18 » or keep the token. Document the trust h2 as a deliberate « caption heading » or move it to an eyebrow-style label.

### QA-28 · nit · fonts and weights
**What:**
- The `01-fonts.css` header comment still describes Barlow Condensed as the label face, and 3 Barlow `@font-face` rules remain even though no rule uses them.
- `--font-condensed` is still referenced, in `form.css` (see QA-20), `footer.css` and `hub.css`.
- Weights 500 and 600 are declared (reassurance items, card titles, FAQ, labels, `strong`), but Scania Sans ships only 400 and 700. So 500 renders as 400 and 600 renders as 700.

**Where:** `src/styles/01-fonts.css`, `00-tokens.css`
**Why it matters:** The CSS doesn't say what actually renders, which misleads the next builder.
**Suggested fix:** Delete the Barlow faces and update the comment. Replace `--font-condensed` with `--font-label`. Map `--fw-medium` to 400 and `--fw-semibold` to 700, or use `--fw-regular` / `--fw-bold` directly.
