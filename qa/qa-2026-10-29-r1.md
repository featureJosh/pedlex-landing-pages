## Summary

The page is in good shape. These checks passed:

- **Overflow:** no horizontal overflow at any of the 6 widths.
- **Console:** no console or page errors on either page.
- **Headings:** exactly one `h1`, and the heading order is clean (h1 → h2 → h3, with no skipped levels).
- **Images:** every image has alt text. The marquee clones are `aria-hidden` with `alt=""`.
- **Indexing:** `noindex, nofollow` is set on both pages.
- **CTAs:**
  - 5 CTAs, all pointing to `#consultation`.
  - At 1440 they work by mouse and keyboard and focus `#lead-name`.
  - Each one pushes `cta_click` with its location.
- **Form:**
  - Validation is accessible: `aria-invalid` plus `aria-describedby`, a summary alert, and focus moves to the first error.
  - A valid submit pushes `{event:"generate_lead", landing:"racking", lang:"fr", form_id:"consultation"}` with **no PII**, disables the button with « Envoi en cours… » and lands on `/racking/merci/`.
  - Without JS, the form still POSTs to `/racking/merci/`.
- **FAQ:** open and close work with mouse, Enter and Space, with a visible 3px focus ring.
- **Carousel:**
  - Prev and Next buttons work, with `aria-disabled` at the ends.
  - ←, →, Home and End work, and the end position rests the last slide exactly on the container edge.
- **Reduced motion:** fully honoured (no drift, static logo row, no counters, `scroll-behavior: auto`).
- **Performance:** CLS was 0.004 on mobile load.
- **Phone links:** every `tel:` link is consistent (`+15143245310`, toll-free `+18887335399`).
- **Contrast:** every text sample measured 5.28:1 or better (the only exception is the decorative counter slash).
- **Alignment:** content aligns to the container edge at every width.
- **Section padding:** consistent: 64 / 84 / 98 / 120 px at 360 / 768 / 1024 / 1440.

**Main problems:** four are conversion or accessibility issues: the mobile CTA lands with the focused field off-screen, the cookie banner hides the hero CTA on phones, the marquee has no pause control for keyboard or touch users, and the thank-you page has a broken icon/eyebrow layout. The rest is consistency debt: one eyebrow bug that misaligns every section by 10–11px, CTA buttons that wrap on phones, phone links built six different ways, and raw values left outside the tokens.

### QA-1 · major · consultation (scroll-to)
**What:** On phones, the CTA scrolls to the *top of the section* (the intro copy) and then focuses `#lead-name` with `preventScroll`. That field sits about 790px below the top of the section:
- At 390×664 (an iPhone with Safari toolbars), the focused field is at y=788 in a 664px viewport, so it is off-screen.
- At 360×640 it is at y=815.
- At 390×844 it only just fits (788–836 of 844).

The visitor sees intro text, not the form, and the cursor sits in a field they cannot see.

**Where:** 390×664 and 360×640. `qa/shots/qa-r1/fn-cta-landing-390x664.png`, `qa/shots/qa-r1/fn-cta-landing-390.png`; numbers in `functional3.log`.

**Why it matters:** "Every CTA scrolls to the form and focuses the first field" is the core conversion promise, and on mobile (the main ad traffic) it doesn't deliver it.

**Suggested fix:** In `src/scripts/components/scroll-to.js`, below 64rem, scroll to the form card instead of the section, for example `(target.querySelector("[data-lead-form]") || target).scrollIntoView(...)`. Alternatively, once scrolling settles, check `field.getBoundingClientRect().bottom > innerHeight` and fall back to `field.scrollIntoView({ block: "center" })`.

### QA-2 · major · cookie banner (global)
**What:** On a first visit, the Law 25 banner is 226px tall at 390 wide:
- At 390×844 its top (y=602) overlaps the hero CTA (y=557–613).
- At 390×664 it covers 36% of the viewport, and the hero CTA is completely hidden behind it; only the phone link peeks out underneath.

It does not close with Escape.

**Where:** 390×844 and 390×664. `qa/shots/qa-r1/fn-cookie-banner-390.png`, `qa/shots/qa-r1/fn-cookie-banner-390x664.png`.

**Why it matters:** The first CTA a paid-ad visitor should see is covered on first paint. PROJECT.md says the banner "never blocks the page or the CTA".

**Suggested fix:** In `components/cookie-banner.css`, below 30rem, make the banner a compact full-width bottom bar:
- `inset: auto 0 0 0` with radius 0 on the bottom corners;
- `padding: var(--space-s)` and text at `--fs-xs`;
- hide the `<h2>` visually (keep it for screen readers);
- buttons side by side.

Aim for 140px or less. Shortening the microcopy is allowed (Luxy microcopy). Optionally let Escape close it as « Refuser ».

### QA-4 · major · trust (logo marquee)
**What:**
- The logo row scrolls forever (a 40s loop) and pauses only on mouse hover.
- Nothing inside `.trust__marquee` can take focus (the measured count of focusable children is 0). So the `:focus-within` pause rule (`trust.css:71`) can never fire.
- Touch and keyboard users have no way to pause it.

**Where:** 1440. `qa/shots/qa-r1/section-trust-1440.png` (mid-scroll; compare the static row in `qa/shots/qa-r1/fn-reduced-motion-trust-1440.png`); `functional2.log` "[marquee]".

**Why it matters:** This fails WCAG 2.2.2 (Pause, Stop, Hide) for auto-moving content longer than 5s, which is an AA requirement in PROJECT.md §6.

**Suggested fix:** Add a small pause/play `<button data-marquee-toggle aria-pressed>` (Lucide `pause`/`play`, 44px target) to `trust.njk`, wired in `trust-marquee.js` by toggling `[data-marquee-paused]` → `animation-play-state: paused`. The `:focus-within` rule then also becomes meaningful.

### QA-5 · minor · global (eyebrow) → hero, problem, process, every section head
**What:**
- `.eyebrow` is `display: inline-flex` inside a block. It therefore sits in an anonymous line box with the body line-height (27px strut), which pushes it **10–11px below the content-box top**.
- Measured: the eyebrow's top is 131px from the section top while `padding-top` is 120px (1440); 74 vs 64 (360).
- In split layouts the eyebrow therefore never lines up with its neighbour:
  - hero eyebrow 156 vs photo 145 (1440);
  - problem cards start 11px above the eyebrow;
  - the process photo starts 11px above the eyebrow.

**Where:** 1024, 1440 and 1920. `qa/shots/qa-r1/section-hero-1440.png`, `qa/shots/qa-r1/section-problem-1440.png`, `qa/shots/qa-r1/section-process-1440.png`; `measure.json` (`eyebrowTop`, `hero`).

**Why it matters:** Every section's top edge is optically misaligned by the same 11px, and it also makes the eyebrow → H2 gap larger than `--space-s`.

**Suggested fix:** In `03-layout.css`, use `.eyebrow { display: flex; width: fit-content; }` and add `margin-inline: auto` for `.section-head--center .eyebrow` (and for `.thanks__inner .eyebrow`).

### QA-6 · minor · problem, process, consultation, faq (CTA buttons)
