## What they sell

**Positioning:** turnkey partner "from A to Z" — analyse, design, install, certify — not just a catalogue.
- Mission: « Aider les entreprises à grandir à l'intérieur même de leurs espaces. » / "Help companies grow within their spaces."
- Vision (2026): « Être le partenaire de confiance qui transforme les espaces en leviers de croissance… »

**Products** (12 categories): cabinets, toolboxes & carts · shelving & mini-racking · workstations · pallet racking (drive-in, push-back, pallet flow) · cantilever · mezzanines · safety & protection / wire-mesh partitions · lifting systems (G-Force, Easy Arm) · modular in-plant offices (ModTech) · lockers (Concorde) · specialized handling · accessories. Also AGVs (Rangerman) and commercial fixtures.
- Lead brand: **Rousseau Metal** (official distributor since 1982, "made in Québec": R/L cabinets, Spider® shelving, Mini-racking, TekZone, MultiTek, R2V, VTZ). Others: Cogan, Metro, VicMobilier, Perfix, Roll Out Racks, Kleton/SCN, MetaGard.

**Services (8):** pallet rack installation & repair · consultation & design (CAD/3D, site scanning) · secure installation (certified teams) · inspection & certification by engineers (CSA A344 / CSA, ANSI, RMI; CNESST-trained; OIQ-sealed) · project management + GC licence · moving & relocation · leasing financing · truck/van outfitting.

**Process line (2026):** "Analyser. Concevoir. Optimiser. Réaliser."

**Offers & CTAs:** request a quote (« Demandez un devis ») · book a showroom visit (Anjou) + 360° virtual tour · online shop **boutique.pedlex.com** (Shopify, in-stock items) · downloadable catalogues · Rousseau configurator (MyModel-r) · monthly newsletter (Mailchimp, "monthly specials") · webinars (5S; machining-tool storage) · expert-advice blog · leasing.

## Who they target

B2B buyers across Québec. Industries: manufacturing · distribution centres · aerospace · automotive (dealership service bays + parts) · healthcare & labs · public sector & schools · commercial/retail · truck fleets (2025 cover also lists food and personal garages).
Buyers: decision-makers who want turnkey service and quality product (scope of work); in practice plant/ops/maintenance managers, dealership parts/service managers, facility & procurement **(inferred)**.
Pains spoken to: lack of space, safety/compliance, planning pitfalls, downtime during install, budget.
